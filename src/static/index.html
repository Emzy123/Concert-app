About the Concert

A powerful night of worship, music and ministry — uniting students and the community in praise.

The annual Light of Life Concert is more than just an event; it's a profound spiritual experience. We gather with one purpose: to lift up the name of Jesus and be filled with His Spirit.

Our goal is to create an atmosphere of pure worship where lives are touched, burdens are lifted, and destinies are reshaped.

Join us as we ignite our hearts and shine His light, bringing revival and hope to CUSTECH Osara and beyond.
